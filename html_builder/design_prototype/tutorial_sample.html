Your first channel: from antenna to impulse response

1. Create an antenna

Every channel starts with antennas. The library ships predefined models; here we use a 3GPP panel with 2 × 2 elements at 3.7 GHz.

In [1]
import quadriga_lib
import numpy as np

ant = quadriga_lib.arrayant.generate('3gpp', M=2, N=2, freq=3.7e9)
print(quadriga_lib.arrayant.calc_directivity(ant, 0))
9.8217

3. A formula

Formulas are written in LaTeX in the notebook and rendered at build time. The relative permittivity of a material at frequency f is

ε(f)=arb−j17.98σf+εpole,σ=crd

with r=f/fRef.
