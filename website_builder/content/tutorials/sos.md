Random fields give every position in space a random value, with values at nearby positions alike; they describe shadow fading in radio channel models, and also quantities such as the height of the sea surface or the hydraulic conductivity of aquifers. This series is for engineers and scientists who need such fields in a simulation: it shows how Quadriga-Lib generates them as a sum of sinusoids, directly at any position and without a grid, and how far the result can be trusted. Part 1 assumes no prior knowledge of random fields and uses the built-in presets; part 2 builds on it and is for readers who need a correlation function that no preset has.
