%%GAP: Draft series introduction: Random fields give every position in space a random value, with nearby values alike; in radio channel models they describe shadow fading and the other large-scale parameters that vary along a route. This series explains how such fields are described by their autocorrelation function and how the sum-of-sinusoids generator of Quadriga-Lib produces them at any position, in one, two or three dimensions. The first part uses the built-in presets; the second fits the generator to a correlation function of your own and shows where the method reaches its limits.%%
