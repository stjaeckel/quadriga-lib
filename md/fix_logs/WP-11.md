## Per item

**B-021 (floors shift all delays).** `get_channels_planar` is now called with `Rz` (3 m per floor) for the probe and
for the actual antennas, so its `dist_rx_tx` is the same 3-D distance from which `path_length` is built. With the
relative delays the function returns, the first tap stays at 0 for any number of floors; the coefficients are
unchanged (the path length was already 3-D). The returned station position has z = 3 m per floor, so absolute delays
computed from `tx_pos` / `rx_pos` include the 3-D distance. Floors add the floor loss (and the TGah shadow fading
below 1 GHz), not delay, as in TGah. C-5: `n_floors = 4: delay of tap 0 = 0.000 ns, tap 1 = 10.000 ns` (before
26.685 / 36.685 ns).

**B-022 (absolute pruning threshold).** The threshold `1e-14` now applies to the normalised PDP (unit sum), before
path loss and shadow fading; the large-scale gain is multiplied in afterwards. Only taps with zero power in the tables
(and the LOS slot without LOS) are removed, so the number of paths no longer depends on distance, floors, walls or
the shadow-fading draw, and strong-loss links do not throw. C-6: 41 or 42 paths at every distance and floor count
(before: 2 paths at 100 m with 2 floors, exceptions with 4 floors).

**B-023 (uplink conjugation) and the downlink Doppler sign.** Probe (`probe/doppler_sign.cpp`, model A, LOS,
station at 3 m approaching the AP at 5 km/h, 2.4 GHz, 1 ms): `get_channels_spherical` along the returned track gives
a LOS phase step of +0.0699 rad per snapshot in both directions; the old IEEE code gave -0.0699 rad in the downlink
and +0.0699 rad in the (conjugated) uplink. Cause: the phasor `exp(-j 2 pi f t)` with `mu = cos(local AoA)` of the
arrival direction (which points towards the source), while the coefficients of the library are proportional to
`exp(-j k d)`, so a shrinking path must rotate as `exp(+j 2 pi f t)`. Changes: the sub-path phasor is
`exp(+j 2 pi f t)` in both loops (probe power and coefficients); the uplink stores the plain transpose (comment at
the former line 710 updated). After the fix: downlink and uplink +0.0699 rad, as `get_channels_spherical`; the
Catch2 test matches `get_channels_spherical` along the track for 10 snapshots to < 1e-3 and the first phase step to
1e-6 rad in both directions. C-7: `max |H_ul^T - H_dl| = 0.000e+00`, `phase step ... downlink +0.1477 rad, uplink
+0.1477 rad` (before 1.5e-3, -0.1480 / +0.1480). The environment Doppler is symmetric, so its statistics do not
change; the station (Jakes) term and the vehicle spike now have the physical sign (TGn: the spike is at positive
Doppler). For a fixed seed the time series of a time-variant channel is the time-reversed one (up to the per-tap
normalisation, which averages over the snapshots); static channels (one snapshot) are bit-identical. The helper
`calc_Doppler_profile` of the Catch2 IEEE tests now uses an FFT over time (IFFT over frequency as before), so the
vehicle test still finds the spike at +89 Hz; the tests that use `abs(f)` are unchanged.

**B-064 (XPR = 0).** `qd_ieee_indoor_param` throws `std::invalid_argument` for `XPR_NLOS_linear` that is not NAN and
not finite and > 0 (0, negative, +-Inf; Inf gave alpha = NaN as well). NAN keeps selecting the model default (all
wrappers pass NAN when the argument is omitted). Behaviour change: a negative value used to restore the default.
C-13 now aborts with `XPR_NLOS_linear must be NAN (model default) or a finite value larger than 0.` (the repro does
not catch).

**B-065 (float-to-integer cast).** `observation_time` and `update_rate` must be finite, and
`floor(observation_time / update_rate)` must be below 2^32 - 1; otherwise `std::invalid_argument`. C-14 (IEEE part):
`observation_time = inf: exception: Observation time must be finite and cannot be negative.` (same for nan); no
sanitizer report. The `quantize` lines of C-14 (`qd_tools_calc_angular_spread.cpp:42-43`) are unchanged and go to
WP-13 with `calc_delay_spread`.

**Performance.** One typical call (model D, 4 x 4 ULA, 3 users, 101 snapshots, station 3 km/h and environment
Doppler, 1 thread), alternating old / new, 3 x 20 calls under `flock /tmp/bench.lock`, load 3.5 before and 2.0

## Behaviour changes for users

- `get_channels_ieee_indoor` with `uplink = true`: the coefficients are the plain transpose of the downlink
  coefficients (before: the conjugate transpose). The uplink now has the same Doppler shift as the downlink.
- Time-variant channels (`observation_time > 0`): every sub-path rotates as `exp(+j 2 pi f t)` (before
  `exp(-j 2 pi f t)`). A station moving towards the AP or a scatterer now sees a positive Doppler shift, as the
  deterministic channel of `get_channels_spherical` along the returned positions; the vehicle spike of model F is at
  +f_spike. With the same seed, the snapshot sequence is the time-reversed one of before (slightly different
  amplitudes through the per-tap normalisation); static channels are unchanged. Doppler spectra computed with an
  IFFT over time (as in the report 11-25-2318) now appear mirrored; use an FFT over time.
- `n_floors > 0`: the tap delays no longer shift with the number of floors (before: +2.8 ns at 1 floor up to
  +26.7 ns at 4 floors for a 5 m link); floors add attenuation (and the TGah shadow fading) only.
- The number of paths no longer depends on distance, shadow fading, floors or walls: strong-loss links keep all taps
  (before: fewer taps, i.e. a smaller delay spread, and an exception when all taps fell below 1e-14).
- `XPR_NLOS_linear`: 0, negative values and +-Inf throw `std::invalid_argument` (before: 0 gave NaN coefficients,
  negative values restored the default 2). NAN still selects the default.
- `observation_time` / `update_rate`: non-finite values, and more than 2^32 - 1 snapshots, throw
  `std::invalid_argument` (before: undefined behaviour, one snapshot for Inf, an Armadillo error for NaN).

## Found on the way

- `tap_spacing_s` is only checked for `round(10 ns / tap_spacing)` being a power of two
  (`src/ieee_channel_model_functions.hpp:81-83`): 7 ns is accepted and treated as 10 ns, 6 ns as 5 ns, although the
  documentation (and the report) say it must equal `10 ns / 2^k`. Not fixed (S4, input validation).
- Jones matrix of the NLOS sub-paths (`src/ieee_channel_model_functions.hpp:531-572`): in the circular basis
  `U M U^H = diag(alpha + beta, alpha - beta)`, i.e. no cross-circular coupling, and the two handednesses get powers
  that differ by `((alpha + beta) / (alpha - beta))^2` = 33.97 (15.3 dB) at XPR = 2 for every sub-path. Circularly
  polarised antennas therefore see a handedness-dependent path gain. The report claims `XPR_circ = XPR` (see the
  publication check, claim 3.6-d). Model question, not changed here.
- Other non-finite inputs of `get_channels_ieee_indoor` are not validated (by reading): NaN `CarrierFreq_Hz` passes
  the `<= 0.1e9` check, NaN `Dist_m` passes `<= 0` (all taps then fail the threshold and the call throws the
  misleading "same number of columns" error of `get_channels_planar`), NaN or Inf speeds and `Doppler_effect` reach
  the phase computation. Not fixed (outside the items).
- The probe normalisation divides by the probe power without the guard the report describes (`g_p = 0` when the
  power is 0, `src/get_channels_ieee_indoor.cpp:541`); after B-064 the probe power is positive for every accepted
  input, so this is not reachable.
- The fluorescent modulation is also applied to static channels (one snapshot, `t = 0`): the 20/50/110 ns taps of
  model E (140/200/290 ns of model D) get a random factor `1 + alpha g(0)`. This is why the delay spreads of models
  D and E in Table 4-1 vary from call to call while B, C and F are deterministic. The report says the mechanism is
  active for `N_snap >= 1`, so this is by design; noted because it is not obvious.

Why the versions differ:
- `4720a92` → `d0f4a31` (WP-01 to WP-10): no difference in any printed value (the full outputs differ only in
  the 16th digit of a Jones-matrix check). The IEEE sources are identical; `get_channels_planar` changed only in its
  documentation; the generic antenna interpolation that it uses changed only for NaN angles (B-007); the
  double-precision baseband phase (B-020, WP-04) and the AVX2 sin/cos change (WP-08) do not change the results at the
  printed precision because the phases involved (delays up to 1 us, 100 MHz, the Doppler grids) are small.
- `d0f4a31` → final (WP-11): only the time-variant results change, through the Doppler sign (B-023 decision): with
  the report's script the Jakes peak (Fig. 4.3) and the vehicle spike (Fig. 4.4) move to negative frequencies; with
  an FFT over time they are at the published positive frequencies. The symmetric spectra (Fig. 4.2, 4.5) change only
  within Monte-Carlo noise: the same seed now gives the time-reversed realisation, `|sum c_s D(k - phi_s)|^2` instead
  of `|sum conj(c_s) D(k - phi_s)|^2` per bin, and the per-tap normalisation averages over that series. Static
  results (Tables 4-1, 4-2) are bit-identical: they have one snapshot (phasor 1), no floors, no walls, and no path
  fell under the old absolute threshold at 4.99 m or 50 m.
- The report's own deviations from the reference rows of Table 4-1 are explained by claim 4.1-c, those of Fig. 4.5
  are not explained (parameters of the published run unknown).

### Blast radius on the report

- **B-023 (plain transpose).** None of the report's results uses uplink channels: every call in sections 4.1-4.3
  uses the default `uplink = false`. Delay spreads and capacities do not depend on the conjugation in any case.
  Effect: none on the results; section 3.9 (formula `H_UL = H_DL^H` and the per-element conjugate) now differs from
  the code.
- **Doppler sign (decided with B-023 on 2026-10-20).** Affects the downlink time series, so all four Doppler
  figures: with the report's MATLAB script (`ifft2` over time) Figures 4.3 and 4.4 appear mirrored (peak at
  -10.9 Hz, spike at -88.6 Hz), Figures 4.2 and 4.5 are statistically unchanged; with an FFT over time the published
  shapes and positive peaks are reproduced. Section 3.8 (phasor `exp(-j 2 pi f t)`) now differs from the code.
  Tables 4-1 and 4-2 are unaffected (static channels).
- **B-021 (floors, no delay).** The report has no multi-floor results. Section 3.3 (attenuation, sigma_F) is
  unaffected. Only the delays with `n_floors > 0` change (the first tap is now at 0, before at 2.8-26.7 ns for 5 m);
  an RMS delay spread is invariant to a common shift, so even multi-floor delay spreads would not change; quantised
  taps and baseband phase slopes would.
- **B-022 (pruning on the normalised PDP).** No path of the report's scenarios (4.99 m and 50 m, no floors or
  walls) fell under the old absolute threshold (results bit-identical). Delay spreads of strong-loss links (large
  distance with floors or walls; e.g. model F, 100 m, 2 floors: 2 instead of 41 paths before) would have been too
  small with the old code.
- **B-064, B-065.** Input validation only; no effect on the report.
