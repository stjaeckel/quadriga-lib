I am working on the acoustic add-on of the thin-sheet composite and the traced-solid slab series in my ray-tracing engine. The engine is built for waves and must stay correct for EM; acoustics is an add-on that has to be accurate on the reflect side and make reasonable predictions on the transmit side. The work is split along the mass column: every material without it (m = 0) is wave physics, settled and guarded; every material with it (m > 0) is acoustic and is being redesigned. One solver, qd_stack_resolve, resolves every sheet and every ordered stack; qd_slab_resolve is the series factor of a traced solid. No acoustic materials are defined for the library yet; this round builds the machine, not the parameters. The goal is that every quantity a published acoustic material specifies (transmission loss and room-side absorption per octave band) can be reached by some setting of the material columns, as a sheet and as a traced solid.

This is the continuation of acoustic round 1. Phases 1 (research, materials) and 2 (candidates as real code, marker runs) are done and written up in md/acoustic_round1_report.md, sections 0 to 2. This session does phase 3: the expressibility tables, the re-examination of the open items, and the decision proposals.

Traps learned in this round (besides md/wave.md section 11): build.sh needs absolute output paths; pkill -f kills the calling shell; two traced solids with a cavity explode the outside-only walker. The early "no tf: raw face" return in qd_stack_resolve's BND runs before anything placed after it. A face's absorption next to a lossy medium is not 1 - |r|^2 (use what it passes over what a pass-all face passes).
